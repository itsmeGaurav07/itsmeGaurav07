## About Me

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate focused on digital hardware design, FPGA architectures, and semiconductor engineering.

- Designing digital systems using Verilog HDL.
- Exploring RTL simulation, functional verification, and computer architecture.
- Developing FPGA-based image-processing and hardware-acceleration projects.
- Expanding my knowledge of SystemVerilog and design verification.
- Interested in RTL Design, Design Verification, FPGA Engineering, and VLSI.

> My goal is to understand not only how hardware works, but how to design, verify, and optimize it.
